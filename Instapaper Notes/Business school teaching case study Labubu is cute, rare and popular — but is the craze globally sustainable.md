> The spectacle starts before the purchase: exclusive product launches create anticipation, social media stories build obsession and the unboxing moment generates emotion. In the blind‑box format each purchase is part toy, part gamble. It is a dynamic that goes beyond physical retail, one that Pop Mart has turned into a marketing machine. Each product launch is treated like a cultural event. Limited‑edition releases, artist collaborations, influencer shout‑outs and vending machines known as Robo Shops all contribute. Collectors do not just buy a figure — they join a tribe whose members, as one analyst noted, place less value in the object than in the experience of hunting, acquiring and sharing it.
