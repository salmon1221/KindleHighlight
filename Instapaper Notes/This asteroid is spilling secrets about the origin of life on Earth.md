> The mystery of life’s origins is not a missing magic ingredient—thanks to studies of meteorites, lab experiments, telescope observations, and now sample return, we know that simple organic molecules form readily throughout the cosmos. The real mystery is the process by which those organic molecules organize into life, and the environment that enables this transformation. [↗](https://www.instapaper.com/read/1957238238/26381589)
