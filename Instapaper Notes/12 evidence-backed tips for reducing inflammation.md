> A study in a 2024 issue of the journal Brain, Behavior, and Immunity found that spending more frequent, pleasant time in nature was associated with lower levels of three different inflammatory markers (interleukin-6, C-reactive protein, and fibrinogen) in the body, among 1,244 U.S. adults.
