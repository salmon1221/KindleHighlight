> The worst part of Trump’s Greenland posture, at least so far, is the rhetoric that it uses. Not only does it openly threaten an ally, but it argues, in a very naked fashion, that might makes right. This message is not only unsettling Europeans. It is also heard loud and clear in Moscow and Beijing. Why should Xi hold back in blackmailing Taiwan when Trump threatens the use of military force against Denmark, a treaty ally and one of the most unwarlike countries on Earth? Why should Putin not go for broke in Ukraine when Trump sends special forces to capture the president of another country and his wife, and puts them on trial in an American court?
